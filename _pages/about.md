Hello! I'm Ali, a PhD student of computer science at the University of Colorado Boulder, advised by Alexis Palmer and Katharina von der Wense. I got my bachelor's degree in compuer engineering from Sharif University of Technology in 2022. Currently, I work on natural language processing, with a focus focus on machine translation and the automatic evaluation and quality estimation of translations, particularly for low-resource languages. 

In my free time, I mainly [watch movies](https://letterboxd.com/alimarashian/list/top-10-1/) (or [make them!](https://letterboxd.com/film/this-is-also-sooth/)) or [read mostly literary books](https://app.thestorygraph.com/five_star_reads/alimarashian).
